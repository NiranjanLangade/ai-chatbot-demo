# Brightpath Academy — Knowledge Base (fictional demo data)

Brightpath Academy is a coaching institute in Pune, Maharashtra, preparing students for JEE (Main & Advanced), NEET and Class 11–12 board exams (CBSE and Maharashtra State Board).

## Contact & location
- Address: 2nd Floor, Sunrise Plaza, FC Road, Shivajinagar, Pune 411005
- Phone / WhatsApp: +91 98765 43210 (demo number)
- Email: admissions@brightpath.example
- Office hours: Monday–Saturday, 9:00 AM – 7:00 PM. Closed on Sundays and public holidays.

## Courses
### JEE Two-Year Program (Class 11 + 12)
- For students entering Class 11. Covers Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics for JEE Main & Advanced plus board syllabus.
- Batch size: maximum 40 students.
- Timings: Weekday batch 4:00 PM – 7:30 PM (Mon–Fri) or Weekend batch 9:00 AM – 3:00 PM (Sat–Sun).
- Fee: ₹1,40,000 per year (₹2,80,000 total). Can be paid in 4 instalments per year.

### NEET Two-Year Program (Class 11 + 12)
- Covers Physics, Chemistry and Biology for NEET plus board syllabus.
- Batch size: maximum 40 students.
- Timings: same as JEE batches.
- Fee: ₹1,30,000 per year (₹2,60,000 total). Can be paid in 4 instalments per year.

### One-Year Crash Course (JEE or NEET, Class 12 / repeaters)
- Intensive revision + 30 full-length mock tests.
- Timings: 9:00 AM – 2:00 PM, Monday–Saturday.
- Fee: ₹95,000.

### Foundation Course (Class 8, 9, 10)
- Builds Maths and Science concepts for school exams and early competitive preparation (Olympiads, NTSE).
- Timings: 5:00 PM – 7:00 PM, three days a week.
- Fee: ₹45,000 per year.

## Admissions
- Admission is through the Brightpath Admission Test (BAT), held every Sunday at 10:00 AM at the FC Road centre. It is free.
- Registration for BAT: call or WhatsApp the admissions number, or fill the enquiry form on the website.
- New batches start in April and June. Crash course starts in May.
- Documents needed: last exam marksheet, Aadhaar card copy, 2 passport-size photos.

## Scholarships
- Based on BAT score: 90%+ → 50% fee waiver, 80–89% → 30% waiver, 70–79% → 15% waiver.
- Additional 10% sibling discount if a brother or sister is currently studying at Brightpath.
- Scholarships cannot be combined beyond a maximum total of 50%.

## Facilities
- Air-conditioned classrooms, a library and a self-study room open 8:00 AM – 9:00 PM.
- Weekly tests every Sunday, with results shared with parents on WhatsApp.
- Doubt-solving sessions daily, 7:30 PM – 8:30 PM.
- Online recorded lectures through the Brightpath student app for revision.
- No hostel. The office can share a list of nearby PG accommodations.

## Policies
- Refund: 100% refund if cancelled within 7 days of admission. After 7 days, fees are non-refundable but can be transferred to a sibling within the same year.
- Attendance: minimum 80% attendance is required to appear in mock test series.
- Parent–teacher meetings happen once every two months.

## Results (fictional)
- 2025: 42 students qualified JEE Advanced, 118 students scored 600+ in NEET.
